# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Cyber World University (CWU) is committed to providing a respectful, welcoming, and constructive environment for everyone who participates in the project.

## Expected Behavior

Contributors and community members should:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Welcome people with different experience levels.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Focus on technical issues rather than individuals.
- Respect project maintainers, contributors, learners, and community members.
- Avoid sharing private or sensitive information.
- Use cybersecurity knowledge responsibly and only in authorized environments.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Discrimination or hateful conduct
- Threats or intimidation
- Deliberate disruption of the project
- Publishing another person's private information
- Malicious or unauthorized security activity
- Spam or abusive behavior
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual content
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, close, or reject contributions or interactions that violate this code of conduct.

Serious or repeated violations may result in restrictions on participation.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer privately rather than escalating the situation publicly.

Reports should include enough context to understand what happened while avoiding unnecessary disclosure of personal information.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces and interactions associated with Cyber World University, including repository contributions, issue discussions, pull requests, and other project-managed community spaces.
